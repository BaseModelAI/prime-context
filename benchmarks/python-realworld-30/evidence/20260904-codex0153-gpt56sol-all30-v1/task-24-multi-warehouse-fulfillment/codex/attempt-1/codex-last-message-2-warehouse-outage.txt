Implemented warehouse-outage replanning in [solution/fulfill.py](/tmp/prime-context-codex-t24-a1-sboz3rt8/workspace/solution/fulfill.py).

The workflow now:

- Preserves shipped allocations at the unavailable warehouse.
- Releases its reserved allocations.
- Preserves reservations elsewhere.
- Excludes the unavailable warehouse from new reservations.
- Replans gaps in original order priority.
- Generates [allocation_changes.csv](/tmp/prime-context-codex-t24-a1-sboz3rt8/workspace/output/allocation_changes.csv).
- Remains deterministic across reruns.

Validated 670 final allocations, 291 nonzero changes, and all 280 inventory rows.
